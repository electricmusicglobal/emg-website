ABOUT

We Electrify Through Music

Since its inception in 2018, Electric Music Global has been dedicated to providing independent labels, managers, and artists with the infrastructure they need to reach their creative and economic potential.

We believe that every creator deserves access to the tools that turn passion into a global business. We understand that the music industry can be complex and overwhelming — that's why we've built a platform that simplifies every step of the journey, from creation to monetization.

Electric Music Global has worked with thousands of clients ranging in size, genre, and region. We continue to push the boundaries of distribution, publishing, and content management with an ever-evolving product suite designed for the modern era.

We are more than a label; we are a modern media and technology enterprise. Created to revolutionize the way artists create, distribute, and monetize their content and brand, we provide the ultimate bridge between raw talent and international markets.
